Tag Sale Benefiting Paint Dunedin Purple

Saturday November 21st

Find a Treasure. Show Your Support. A tag sale benefiting Paint Dunedin Purple Love the thrill of a great find? Give your next treasure hunt a purpose! Come out to our tag sale benefiting Paint Dunedin Purple and turn a little shopping into meaningful community support.

Why come out? Because community support starts with people showing up. Your visit is a chance to shop with purpose, connect with neighbors, and help make this fundraiser a success. Bring a friend, spread the word, and invite someone who loves a good tag sale as much as you do. You don’t need a big gesture to be part of something meaningful. Browse, shop, and help us rally support for Paint Dunedin Purple. Come for the finds. Stay for the community. Show up for Paint Dunedin Purple!

History of Paint Dunedin Purple Paint Dunedin Purple (PDP) was created nearly 9 years ago by Steve Olson, a Dunedin resident who moved to raise awareness and money for the Alzheimer's Association after his mother was diagnosed with Alzheimer’s. After the first year of flying solo, he connected with local business owners to engage them in his efforts. The first year we raised nearly $10,000 on The Longest Day. Since then, The Alzheimer’s Association has rebranded The Longest Day into Do What You Love to End ALZ. In 2026, over 80 businesses participated, raising more than $50,000. Businesses held a variety of activities, from just wearing purple to serving purple food and drinks, holding educational opportunities, pub crawls, and other fundraisers. Dunedin really gets involved in Paint Dunedin Purple. A sea of purple is seen on the day of the event.

Books & Brews

Starting on Tuesday December 1st 2026

Monthly on every first Tuesday

Cueni Brewing Co. hosts a book club for people who love to read and want to discuss what they've read while enjoying a beer or two. The format is simple: each month, a volunteer leads the discussion.

To lead, you must have read the book, be willing to ask questions, and lead the discussion.

Everyone is welcome, even if you haven’t finished the book; we want you to join us. We meet the first Tuesday of the month from 7:00 - 8:00 pm.
