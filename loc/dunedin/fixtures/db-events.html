Concerts & Events

***All Music Events are Free Admission Unless Otherwise Noted***

Dunedin Brewery is a staple of the live music scene in Tampa Bay, hosting a fusion of up and coming touring regional acts along with support for local talent. 

Since it began in the mid 2000s, our Wednesday Night Open Jam has become a staple of the Tampa Bay music scene--known for spawning new successful bands. Fridays & Saturday evenings find both local and touring musicians on one of our two stages. Wrap up the weekend with Sunday Service, an afternoon session outdoors when weather permits.

We also host a few mini-music festivals including Oktobeerfest, Spring Beer Jam, Joe Marcinek's Snowbird Jamboree, & more.

All music & entertainment is booked in-house. If you are interested in performing at Dunedin Brewery, all inquiries must be sent to mailto:talent@dunedinbrewery.com

Tasty Vibrations

Friday, November 20 @ 8:00PMFri, Nov 20 @ 8:00PM

Tasty Vibrations is an award-winning, eclectic alternative jam band hailing from Pompano Beach, Florida. Formed in 2018, the five-piece group seamlessly blends a high-energy "Fort Lauderdale beach sound" with strong influences of reggae, funk, hip-hop, and psychedelic rock. Heavily inspired by South Florida's melting pot of cultures, their signature style fuses a laid-back, sun-soaked island vibe with a heavy dose of live improvisation, ensuring that no two live sets are ever exactly the same.
