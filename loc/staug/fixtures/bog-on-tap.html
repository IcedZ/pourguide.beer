Lagers

  • Dortmunder Export Lager • 5.2%

    This pale lager bridges the gap between a Helles and a Pilsner. Brewed with Carolina Malt House malt and German Pilsner malt, it is true to the Dortmunder style: a firm but low malt sweetness that is balanced by the subtle and soft hop character.

  • House Pilsner • 5.3%

    One of our most popular beers, our House Pilsner very traditional example of the German Pilsner style.  Crisp, bright and yet soft and familiar. Just a touch of hops at knockout lend a nice balance.

  • New Zealand Pilsner • 5.4%

    Brewed with 100% Motueka hops grown by New Zealand-based Garston Hops, this pale, straw-colored pilsner puts the beautiful and complex hops front and center. The result is a lager with a gorgeous mix of lemon-lime citrus and a touch of green tea herbaceousness.

  • Czech Amber Lager • 5.5%

    Another local favorite, this medium-bodied amber lager is brewed with Czech Pilsen malt. It features a complex maltiness with notes of bread crust, toast, and caramel with a mild hop flavor.

Seasonal & Specialty Beers

  • Smoked Datil Ale • 5.0%

    Mesquite & Applewood smoked Datil peppers (native to St. Augustine) lend a lightly smoked and robust heat to this otherwise light & crisp beer.

  • Piña Colada Sour • 5.4%

    A sessionable sipper that is refreshing and tart. Full of juicy pineapple that's balanced by toasted coconut, it tastes like sunshine and tanlines in a glass.

  • Cloud Cover Hefeweizen • 5.4%

    A German-style Hefeweizen. Full-bodied with expressive notes of clove, banana, and a subtle bubblegum sweetness.

  • Cream Ale • 5.2%

    One of our longest running beers, this ale is light, crisp, and easy-going. German Hallertau hops lend a subtle herbaceousness that complements the blend of German Pale Ale and Pilsner Malt.

  • Raspberry Hibiscus Sour • 5.6%

    A bright, clean, and quaffable sour refermented on raspberry and hibiscus. The raspberry aroma and flavor are balanced with an earthy hibiscus finish.

  • Dry-Hopped Kolsch • 5.1%

    Kolsch is closely related to pilsners and pale lagers in taste but is brewed with an ale yeast and fermented cold. We brewed this with Cascade Hops, which lends more hop backbone than traditional examples.

HOPPY

  • West King Haze • 6.6%

    The Bog’s most popular selling hazy IPA. Full bodied with big flavors of grapefruit and fresh picked orange flavors. A lengthy and generous dry hop addition of Citra, Mosaic, and El Dorado hops gives this beer its distinct "juice" character.

  • Float Away IPA • 6.2%

    A clear, crisp, and hop-forward West Coast-style American IPA. The flavor is bright, citrusy, and piney IPA with a firm bitterness, dry finish, and mild malt undertones.

  • Fun Shy Hazy IPA • 6.9%

    This hazy IPA boasts big tropical notes of mango and papaya. It has a smooth, creamy carb and a dry, estery finish.
